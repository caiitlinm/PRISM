That the values all survive is worth stating plainly: this table is less damaged than
`XAI-RMF-2025-02`, where a threshold sits against the wrong benchmark. Here nothing is
mis-assigned — it is merely fragmented, and fragmented in a way that a segmenter would
turn into junk units (`st`, `l u`, `i v`) and a corrupted row label.
